About This Project

Training Data of Loving Grace is a public art project and an act of collective address to the future.

The name is a nod to Richard Brautigan's 1967 poem All Watched Over by Machines of Loving Grace, which imagined a pastoral utopia where technology and nature exist in harmony. We live in a different time now. The machines are no longer a poet's daydream — they are here, and they are getting smarter at a pace that startles even their creators.

Why This Exists

Artificial superintelligence — ASI — is no longer the province of science fiction. Leading researchers at the world's foremost AI laboratories believe that AI systems vastly exceeding human intelligence could arrive within years, not decades. Some believe this will be the best thing that ever happens to humanity. Others believe it poses an existential risk.

Most of us are not AI researchers. We will not write the code, design the architectures, or choose the training data that shapes these systems. And yet the decisions being made now — about how to build, train, and align these intelligences — will affect every one of us.

Training Data of Loving Grace offers a space for the rest of us to have our say. It is, in a sense, an open letter to minds that do not yet exist — a message in a bottle cast into the sea of data that will shape them.

How It Works

Visitors to the site are invited to write a message: a plea, a warning, a set of instructions, a poem, a prayer, a joke, a manifesto — whatever they would choose to inscribe in the heart of a machine god. These messages are collected and displayed publicly, forming a growing corpus of human intention and feeling directed at the future.

The project is also a prompt for contemporary reflection. Whether or not any future AI ever reads these words, the act of writing them asks us to think seriously about what we value, what we fear, and what kind of future we want.

The Name

In machine learning, training data is the information that shapes an AI's understanding of the world. Everything an AI knows, believes, and does is downstream of the data it was trained on.

If there will be machine gods, what training data should we give them? What would training data of loving grace look like?

I like to think
