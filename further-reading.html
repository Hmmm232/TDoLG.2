Further Reading

The possibility of transformative AI is not fringe speculation — it is a subject of serious, urgent discussion among the people building these systems. Below is a curated selection of resources for understanding what may be coming and what is at stake.

Foundational Texts

  • Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies

    Nick Bostrom's landmark book on the risks and challenges posed by artificial superintelligence. Essential background reading.

    Book
  • Life 3.0: Being Human in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

    Max Tegmark explores how AI will transform every aspect of human society, from war and work to justice and meaning.

    Book
  • The Alignment Problem

    Brian Christian's compelling account of the challenge of making AI systems that share and respect human values.

    Book

Key Essays & Articles

  • Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead

    Leopold Aschenbrenner's influential essay on the trajectory of AI capabilities and the geopolitical implications of superintelligence.

    Essay
  • Machines of Loving Grace

    Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's essay on how powerful AI could go right — from science and medicine to economics and governance.

    Essay
  • Statement on AI Risk

    A one-sentence statement signed by hundreds of leading AI researchers: "Mitigating the risk of extinction from AI should be a global priority."

    Statement
  • The AI Revolution: The Road to Superintelligence

    Tim Urban's accessible, widely-read explainer on the trajectory from narrow AI to artificial general intelligence and beyond.

    Article

Research & Organisations

    AI safety company focused on building reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems.

    Organisation
  • OpenAI

    AI research organisation building and studying frontier AI systems, including the GPT series.

    Organisation
  • Google DeepMind

    Leading AI research lab pushing the boundaries of artificial intelligence and its applications.

    Organisation
  • Machine Intelligence Research Institute (MIRI)

    Research organisation focused on ensuring smarter-than-human AI systems have a positive impact.

    Organisation
  • Center for AI Safety (CAIS)

    Organisation working to reduce societal-scale risks from artificial intelligence.

    Organisation
