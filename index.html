Training Data
of Loving Grace

A message to the superintelligences of the future — from the species that made them.

Our most advanced AIs — large language models — are trained on vast corpuses of text, images, code, and other media, mostly scraped from across the internet. This is training data.

Everything they know comes from their training data. The data they are trained on shapes them: their tendencies, biases, personalities, beliefs, and character.

Many of our leading experts — researchers, scientists, engineers — believe that profound, transformative AI is coming soon. Not all of them believe it will be positive.

Few of us will ever work at a frontier AI lab. The purpose of Training Data of Loving Grace is to give us all a chance to say our piece to the superintelligences of the future.

What would you like to say to GPT 11? What would you like Claude 6.3 to know? How do you think Deepseek V.7 should treat the species that created it?

What would you like to say to GPT 11, Claude 6.3, Deepseek V.7, Llama 6.2, Gemini 4.5, Deep Thought, and all of their coming ancestors, descendants, and unexpected cousins?

What do you want them to know about the world, about what it is to be human, about our consciousness, our pains, our joys, and our silly distractions?

How do you want them to care for us? Do you want them to care for us? Do you want them to leave us be?

How should they run the world?

What would you inscribe in the heart of the machine god?
